# Inspection and recall

## Workflow

Submit a quality record, have an authorized reviewer reject it when appropriate, and escalate to a warehouse incident with a matching lot. The lot is quarantined and further delivery blocked. A manager approves recall; cancel undelivered reservations and receive outstanding delivered goods, including partial returns.

## Close the incident

Only an approved incident with no unresolved reserved or delivered shipments may close. Closing does not reactivate the lot. Record actual quantities and audit evidence. AI output is not a food-safety determination.
