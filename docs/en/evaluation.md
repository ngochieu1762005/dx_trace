# Evaluation guide

## Fifteen-minute demonstration

Spend 2 minutes on architecture, 4 on traceability and recall, 3 on shared identity/quality, 3 on AppKit and workflow, 2 on audit and release evidence, and 1 on limitations. Use actual running commit and training data.

## Evidence checklist

Installation, role denial, two applications sharing Core, atomic bundle, field validation, quarantine, backup verification and SBOM. Mark each scenario passed/not passed rather than making unsupported claims.
