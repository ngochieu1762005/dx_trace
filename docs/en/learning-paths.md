## Practice safely

Use the training workspace for exercises. Record the running commit, select the correct workspace, and keep credentials and real records out of public evidence.

## Start a warehouse shift

At handover, give the incoming operator one active lot code and its shipment reference. They look it up under their own account, verify the warehouse area and reconcile delivered, reserved or outstanding quantities. If the numbers differ, compare timestamps and individual shipments before editing stock. [Worked reconciliation](hdpi.md).
