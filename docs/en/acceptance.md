# Acceptance

## Full-stack verification

Verify TLS, issuer and callback, two real identities, same-workspace authorization, denial cases, expired sessions, shared portal and business operations. Rehearse restore with warehouse and Keycloak in isolated volumes. Record actual results and environment.

Do not mark production acceptance complete solely because CI passed.
